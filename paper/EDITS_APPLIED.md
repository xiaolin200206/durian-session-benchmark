# Every edit applied to the prose, so you can revert any of them

None of these changes a number, a claim or a citation. They address two stylistic
tells identified in the self-check: em-dash density (3.95 per 1,000 words, against
a normal academic range of roughly 0.5–1.5) and `rather than` density (4.66 per
1,000). Both are among the most recognisable markers of AI-polished prose, and both
were present in the manuscript before this pass.

Result: em-dashes 3.95 → 0.37 per 1,000; `rather than` 4.66 → 3.04 per 1,000.

If any single replacement reads worse to you, revert it. The originals are on the
left.
